## About the Unit

COS40005 is a capstone-style project unit in which students work in teams on projects sourced from industry, research partners or self-sourced topics. Project A covers Weeks 5 to 12 and focuses on initiation, planning, research, delivery of a first working release and handover. Assessment is made up of team and individual portfolio tasks, including a research report, a team and project plan, and an individual contribution summary.

## Team Project

**Internal Assistant for Enterprise Knowledge (Slack App Bot)** is a Retrieval-Augmented Generation assistant that answers employees' questions inside Slack from internal knowledge sources, starting with Confluence. Key features are English and Vietnamese support, source citations, and deny-by-default channel whitelisting with Slack single sign-on validation.

- **Client:** VIB, through the AWS First Cloud Journey (FCAJ) programme
- **Team:** six members, with me as project manager
- **Release in Project A:** Alpha (proof of concept), followed by a Technical Specification Document for the client
- **Schedule:** 29 September 2026 to 6 December 2026

Code, planning documents and the technical details are in the team repository: [Slack_Bot_Project_Brok](https://github.com/hieugia090806/Slack_Bot_Project_Brok).

**Research Report at a glance**
- *Business domain:* enterprise knowledge is fragmented across Confluence spaces, documentation sites and forums
- *End users:* developers, support staff and operations staff who lose time to context switching and are unsure what they may access
- *Solution domain:* a four-layer reference model (Slack interface, data ingestion and indexing, retrieval with access control, augmented generation)
- *Stakeholders and ethics:* internal employees, knowledge managers and administrators, academic evaluators and client mentors; privacy, access control and hallucination risk
